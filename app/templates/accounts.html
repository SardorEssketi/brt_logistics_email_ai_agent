How to get an App Password
  1. Sign in to the Gmail account in your browser and open myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification (Google asks for a phone number).
  3. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type the name Wayflix Trucking Email Sender and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-letter password Google shows (it is shown only once) and paste it above.

If you later change this Gmail's normal password, Google cancels its App Passwords. The account then shows as Stopped here; create a new App Password and paste it under “New App Password”.
